Customer

    Can buy cash
    Can buy on credit
    Can have multiple contacts
    Can have multiple delivery addresses
    Can have a credit limit
    Can be inactive

Sale
    Every sale belongs to one customer.
    A sale has at least one product.
    A sale cannot exceed available stock.
    Discounts require permission.
    Prices cannot be negative.
    Tax is calculated after discounts.
    A completed sale cannot be edited.
    Cancelled sales restore stock.

Payment
    One invoice can have multiple payments.
    Payments may be partial.
    Overpayments create customer credit.
    Payments can be reversed.

Returns
    Returned products go back to stock.
    Some products cannot be returned.
    Returns create a credit note.
